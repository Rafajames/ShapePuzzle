Privacy Policy — Shape Puzzle

Updated November 6, 2026.

1. Who we are

Shape Puzzle is a game created by Jamerson Rafael (an individual, Brazil), who is responsible for the data processed in the game. For requests about your data, please use the Delete my account page first. For other questions, open a conversation at https://github.com/Rafajames/ShapePuzzle/issues without including personal data.

2. Playing without an account

The game works without an account. Your progress, records, statistics, settings and the levels you create stay only on your device.

3. Google account (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Google's Firebase Authentication service receives your account identifier, email address and name, only to identify you. The game uses your first name only as a nickname suggestion, and you choose whatever nickname you like. We store:

  • Public profile: nickname, avatar, frame, avatar background and number of finished levels. Other players can see it (for example, in the overall ranking).
  • Private data (only you can read it): progress, records, statistics, settings, levels you created, your stars, items, achievements and coupons already used, as a backup so you can continue on another device.

We do not collect photos, location, contacts or other data from your device.

4. Championships and ranking

When you join an online championship, the name you type (or your nickname, in the weekly championship) and your time and number of moves are visible to the organizer and the participants. The overall ranking shows the public profile of players who signed in with Google. Offline (QR code) championships send nothing over the internet.

5. Reports, blocking and coupons

When you report a player or a championship, we send the reported name, the reason, the date and, if you are signed in, your account identifier (to prevent abuse). Only the person responsible for the game reads reports, and the reported person does not know who reported them. Blocking someone stays only on your device. When you use a coupon, the game only checks the code; its use is saved on your device and in your backup.

6. Ads (Android version)

The Android version may show optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch them. In that case Google may use the advertising identifier and device data, as described in Google's advertising policy. In Europe and the United Kingdom we ask for your consent first. You can delete or reset the advertising ID in your Android settings.

7. What we do not do

We use no usage analytics or tracking, we build no behavioral profiles and we do not sell your data.

8. Who processes data for us

Google (Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database and, when ads exist, AdMob), which may store data on servers outside Brazil, including in the United States. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses in its own records; the Android app also downloads new versions of the game from there.

9. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your public profile, your private data and the account itself. Data of a championship stays until its organizer deletes it; weekly championship rooms may be deleted after the week ends. Reports are kept until they are reviewed, for at most 12 months.

10. Your rights

You can access and correct your data (My profile → Nickname), delete it (in the game, under My profile → Account and cloud → Delete my account and data, or on the Delete my account page), take a copy (Menu → Backup) and withdraw consent at any time. This applies under Brazil's LGPD and, for people in Europe, the GDPR.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Creating an account requires being 13 or older (or the minimum age in your country). If a minor created an account, request deletion on the Delete my account page.

12. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS) and database rules limit who can read and write each piece of data. No system is completely secure.

13. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for relevant changes, notify you in the game. See also the Terms of Use.

Updated November 6, 2026.

1. Acceptance

Stars, avatars, frames, backgrounds and achievements are virtual. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged for money. Coupons are promotional: each can be used once per account or device, may have an expiry date and may be discontinued. We may adjust prices, rules and balances to fix errors or abuse.

5. Levels and content you create

You are responsible for the levels and names you create and share. Do not share illegal or offensive content.

6. Reports and blocking

In the rankings, the ⋯ button next to each player lets you report or block them; championships have the Report button. We review reports and may remove content and suspend accounts. Blocking hides the person only for you, on this device. You can also tell us at https://github.com/Rafajames/ShapePuzzle/issues (without personal data).

The game is provided “as is”. It may become unavailable or change, and we do not guarantee that cloud progress is never lost; keep a backup too (Menu → Backup).

8. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms; the date above shows the version in force. The laws of Brazil apply.

Delete my account — Shape Puzzle

This page deletes your Shape Puzzle account and the data saved in the cloud, without opening the game.

What will be deleted

  • Your public profile: nickname, avatar, frame, background and number of finished levels.
  • Your cloud backup: progress, records, statistics, settings, created levels and stars.
  • The account itself (Google sign-in for Shape Puzzle).

What stays

  • The progress and stars saved on your device. To remove them, clear the site or app data in your device settings.
  • Championship results you already sent: ask the organizer to delete the championship.

To confirm the account is yours, sign in with Google.
